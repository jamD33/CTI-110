How to Guide a Robot to make a Peanut Butter sandwhich

Assumptions
The Robot is functional, powered on, standing in front of the kitchen counter
The following unopened items are on the counter: one bag of bread, one jar of peanut butter, one jar of jelly
A clean butter knife and a clean plate are on the counter. 
The robots hands are empty.
The robot understands spatial commands 'extend arm', rotate wrist', open grip, and close grip. 

Step-by-step instructions
1. Extend left arm towards bag of bread
2. Position left hand around the plastic clip
3. Close the left hand grip to pinch the plastic clip
4. Pull the left hand away from the bag
5. Open the left hand grip to drop onto the counter
6. Insert the right hand inside the bread bag
7. Close the right hand grip lightly to grab a slice of the bread
8. Pull the right hand out of the bag 
9. Open your right hand to release the bread
10. Insert your right hand back into the bag
11. Grab a second slice of bread
12. Open your right hand grip to release the bread
13. With your right hand reach for the peanut butter jar lid
14. Extend your right hand toward the peanut butter lid
15. Close your right hand tightly around the sides of the lid
16. Close your left hand tightly under the sides of the peanut butter jar
17. Rotate the right wrist counter-clockwise repeatly until the lid is off
18. Open the right hand to release the lid 
19. Reach toward the butter knife
20. Close your right hand tightly around the knife 
21. Move the right hand so the blade of the kife lowers into the peanut butter jar
22. Scoop out approximately 1 tablespoon of peanut butter
23. Life the knife out of the jar
24. Press the flat side of the knife against the first slice of bread
25. Move the knife back and forth until the peanut butter is spread across the surface
26. Repeat steps 14-18 to open the jelly 
27. Scoop out approximately 1 tablespoon of jelly
28. Press the flat side of the kinfe agaisnt the second slice of bread 
29. Move the knife back forth to spread on the surface of the second slice of bread
30. Place the knife flat on the surface next to the plate
31. Reach for the first slice of bread with the peanut butter
32. Reach for the second slice of bread with the jelly
33. Lift the slice of bread with the peanut butter and flip it downward onto the slice of bread with the jelly 
34. Align the slices of bread together

Done when: The robot is finsihed when two slices of bread are stacked directly on top of eachother on the plate.
